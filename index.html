Getting here

Coming from out of town? DC is easy to reach, and everything this weekend is in Dupont Circle or Adams Morgan, a short walk or ride apart.

Flying in

  • Reagan National (DCA) is the closest airport, with a Metro stop right at the terminal.
  • Dulles (IAD) connects to downtown on the Silver Line.
  • Baltimore (BWI) often has cheaper fares. Take the MARC or Amtrak train to Union Station.

Train or bus

Amtrak and most intercity buses arrive at Union Station. From there, the Red Line runs straight to Dupont Circle.

Getting around

For Saturday, take the Red Line to Dupont Circle, like we did on our wedding day. Toolbox is about a block from the station. For Friday, we'll share the exact spot soon. It will be in Adams Morgan, a walk from Dupont Circle or Woodley Park Metro. Parking in both neighborhoods is hard to find.

Our DC

Staying for the weekend? Here are a few places we love.

  • Dupont CircleThe party's neighborhood. Coffee, bookstores, and the Sunday farmers market for the morning after.
  • Adams MorganBars, restaurants, and murals up and down 18th Street, right around the corner from The LINE.
  • Museums on the MallOur favorites are the National Gallery of Art and the Smithsonian's Natural History Museum. Both are free.
  • The National Mall at sunsetWalk from the Capitol to the Lincoln Memorial as the lights come on.
  • Activities for kidsCheck out the National Zoo (free!), Air and Space Museum (free!), Planet Word (free!), National Geographic Museum (worth the fee!).
  • Rock Creek ParkA forest in the middle of the city, for a quiet walk after a late night.

Questions

Wait, you're already married?

Yes! We got married at the DC courthouse on September 25, 2026. This is the party we've been wanting to throw ever since.

What does "outdress the brides" mean?

It means cocktail attire at the very least, and more is better. Sequins, a sharp suit, bold color, a statement jacket: this is the night for it. Just wear shoes you can dance in.

Will there be food at the party?

Yes. Timber Pizza is catering the party, so come hungry. Let us know about any dietary needs on the RSVP form.

Can I bring a plus-one?

If your invitation includes a guest, yes. Just note everyone's names and the number of people coming on the RSVP form.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely. Everyone is welcome, kids included.

What's the weather like in early March?

Usually chilly, and it can swing from cold to mild. Bring a coat you're happy to check at the door.

Is the venue accessible?

There are two steps up to the main door at Toolbox. If that's a challenge for you or someone in your group, email us ahead of time and we'll make sure you're taken care of.

Who do I ask about anything else?
